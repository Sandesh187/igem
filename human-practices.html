Human Practices

Science does not exist in a vacuum. We engaged with the stakeholders of Mumbai's ecosystem to ensure PET-Destroyer is safe, ethical, and effective.

Stakeholder Mapping

We categorized our engagement into four primary sectors to cover the entire lifecycle of our implementation.

Government

Consulted with the BMC regarding wastewater microplastic regulations.

Industry

Interviews with PET recycling plant managers to understand current limitations.

NGOs

Collaborated with 'Vanshakti' to understand plastic impact on marine biodiversity.

Public

Surveyed 500+ residents of Mumbai about their trust in synthetic biology.

THE INTEGRATION CYCLE

How Feedback Shaped Our Lab

The Insight

During an interview with a Marine Biologist, it was pointed out that releasing free-floating bacteria into the Mithi River could lead to unpredictable ecological shifts, even with a kill switch.

Our Response

We pivoted our design from a "direct release" model to an Immobilized Bio-Film system. Our bacteria are now housed within permeable silica beads, ensuring they never enter the open ecosystem.

Education & Community

Empowering the next generation of biotechnologists in India.

We conducted a 3-day workshop at local municipal schools, introducing students to the basics of DNA and the importance of plastic-free oceans. We distributed a "Synthetic Biology Primer" translated into Marathi and Hindi for local accessibility.
