// LAB_JOURNAL_2025

A transparent record of our experiments, failures, and breakthroughs.

JULY 05, 2025

Project Brainstorming

Initial meeting to discuss the "Plastic Crisis" in Mumbai. Decided to focus on V. natriegens due to its superior growth kinetics in high-salinity water environments.

JULY 18, 2025

Primers & G-Blocks Ordered

Finalized the codon-optimized sequence for PETase (Ideonella sakaiensis). Primers arrived and were verified via nanodrop.

AUGUST 02, 2025

Plasmid Assembly (Gibson)

Performed Gibson Assembly for the pET-22b(+) vector containing our PETase insert. Transformation into E. coli DH5α was successful.

AUGUST 14, 2025

SDS-PAGE Analysis

Induced expression with 0.5mM IPTG at 20°C. SDS-PAGE results show a strong band at ~30 kDa, confirming PETase production.
