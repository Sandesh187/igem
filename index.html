PET-DESTROYER

Designing the future of plastic recycling.

We are engineering Vibrio natriegens with a high-efficiency secretome to break down microplastics into renewable carbon sources.

Explore the Design

The Problem

33 Million Tons of PET Annually

Global PET production exceeds 33 million tons annually. Traditional recycling is energy-intensive and leads to "downcycling." Our biological solution targets the monomers directly.

  • ✓ Faster degradation than chemical methods
  • ✓ Lower energy costs at ambient temperatures
  • ✓ Circular economy - monomers can be repolymerized
