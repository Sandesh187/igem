From Lab to Ocean

Our implementation strategy focuses on integrating engineered V. natriegens into existing wastewater infrastructure and open-ocean filtering buoys.

Phase 01: Municipal Integration

Current Mumbai wastewater treatment plants (WWTPs) are not equipped to filter microplastics. We propose the "Bio-Filter Module," a localized bioreactor containing our immobilized bacteria.

  • Continuous flow treatment for high-salinity runoff.
  • Enzymatic breakdown of PET into TPA and Ethylene Glycol.
  • Real-time monitoring via bioluminescent biosensors.

Phase 02: Autonomous Ocean Buoys

For the "Great Pacific Garbage Patch," we envision a fleet of Bioluminescent Buoys. These units use solar energy to pump water through a mesh filter seeded with our PET-Destroyer strain.

Biocontainment & Safety

To prevent the spread of engineered genes, our implementation includes a dual-layered Kill Switch that triggers if the salinity levels drop below marine standards or if specific chemical cues are absent.
