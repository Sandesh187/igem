01. Introduction

Polyethylene terephthalate (PET) is the most abundant polyester in the world. Our project, PET-Destroyer, aims to tackle this by engineering a microbial system that outperforms existing solutions in speed and cost.

02. The Chassis: V. natriegens

We chose Vibrio natriegens because it is the fastest-growing organism known, with a doubling time of ~10 minutes. This allows for rapid production of degradative enzymes compared to traditional E. coli systems.

03. Genetic Design

Our construct utilizes the T7 expression system. We optimized the signal peptides (PelB and OmpA) to ensure the PETase enzyme is secreted into the extracellular space where it can interact with plastic particles.
