Safety & Ethics

Our project operates under Biosafety Level 1 (BSL-1) standards. Below is our comprehensive risk management strategy.

Potential Risk Safety Level Mitigation Strategy
Chassis Escape Low Incorporated a toxin-antitoxin "kill switch" system.
Horizontal Gene Transfer Medium Used non-conjugative plasmids to prevent spread to wild strains.
Lab Contamination Low Standard aseptic techniques and 70% ethanol sterilization.
